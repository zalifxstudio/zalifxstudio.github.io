About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Imam Alif Firmansyah (zalifxstudio) handles information when you use PuzzleSnap ("App") on Android.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

No Account, No Server Storage

PuzzleSnap does not require an account and does not use Google Sign-In, Firebase, or any backend server. There is no leaderboard and no cloud sync. Every bit of your progress stays on your own device.

Data Type Collected By Stored On Sent to Server?
Puzzle progress: completed levels, stars, coins, streaks, best times App Your device (SharedPreferences) No
Settings and preferences App Your device (SharedPreferences) No
Advertising ID, device info, IP, ad interaction and consent data Google AdMob and mediated demand partners (e.g. Unity Ads) Google's / partner servers Yes, by Google/partners

Advertising

The Android version may display advertising supplied by Google AdMob and mediated demand partners such as Unity Ads. These providers may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and consent signals to deliver, measure, secure, or personalize ads as permitted by your choices and applicable law.

Where required, the App presents Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before requesting ads. You can revisit your advertising privacy choices any time from Settings & privacy inside the app.

Rewarded ads are optional. A gameplay reward (such as the "fix one tile" assist) is granted only after the advertising SDK confirms the video was completed. You can also opt out of ad personalization via Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization on your Android device.

Analytics

This build does not send gameplay analytics anywhere. It contains a telemetry interface whose default implementation discards every event on-device. If analytics is enabled in a future release, this policy and the in-app consent behavior will be updated before any collection begins.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information to any third party.
  • We do not operate any server that stores your gameplay data — there is nothing on our side to share.
  • Advertising partners retain information under their own policies and legal obligations.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your local game data stays on your device until you remove it yourself:

  • In-app: Settings & privacy → Reset all progress.
  • Full removal: Uninstall the app, or clear its data via Settings > Apps > PuzzleSnap > Clear Data on your device.

Children's Privacy

PuzzleSnap is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. The advertising configuration does not declare the app as child-directed.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used this app in a way that concerns you regarding advertising, non-personalized ads can be enabled at the operating-system level via Google Family Link.

Security

  • All network traffic used by the app (advertising SDKs) uses encrypted HTTPS connections; cleartext Android traffic is disabled.
  • Because there is no account and no server, there is no password or server-side profile to protect.

Your Rights

  • Access & deletion: Your data lives only on your device — view it in-app, or delete it via Reset all progress / uninstall.
  • Opt-out of ad personalization: Via Settings > Google > Ads on your Android device.
  • Depending on your location, you may have additional rights regarding data processed by our advertising partners — see their policies linked above.

Tentang Kebijakan Ini

Kebijakan Privasi ini menjelaskan bagaimana Imam Alif Firmansyah (zalifxstudio) menangani informasi saat kamu menggunakan aplikasi PuzzleSnap ("Aplikasi") di Android.

Dengan menggunakan Aplikasi, kamu menyetujui praktik yang dijelaskan dalam kebijakan ini.

Tanpa Akun, Tanpa Penyimpanan Server

PuzzleSnap tidak memerlukan akun dan tidak menggunakan Google Sign-In, Firebase, atau server backend apa pun. Tidak ada leaderboard, tidak ada sinkronisasi cloud. Seluruh progresmu tersimpan hanya di perangkatmu sendiri.

Iklan

Versi Android dapat menampilkan iklan dari Google AdMob dan mitra mediasi seperti Unity Ads. Mitra ini dapat memproses device identifier, perkiraan lokasi dari IP address, interaksi iklan, diagnostik, dan sinyal consent untuk menampilkan, mengukur, mengamankan, atau mempersonalisasi iklan sesuai pilihanmu dan hukum yang berlaku.

Jika diwajibkan, Aplikasi menampilkan formulir consent Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) sebelum meminta iklan. Kamu bisa meninjau ulang pilihan privasi iklan kapan saja dari Settings & privacy di dalam aplikasi.

Iklan reward bersifat opsional. Reward gameplay (seperti bantuan "fix one tile") hanya diberikan setelah SDK iklan mengonfirmasi video selesai ditonton. Kamu juga bisa menonaktifkan personalisasi iklan lewat Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization di perangkat Android-mu.

Analitik

Build ini tidak mengirim analitik gameplay ke mana pun. Ada interface telemetri di dalamnya, tapi implementasi default-nya membuang semua event di perangkat sendiri tanpa dikirim keluar.

Berbagi Data

  • Kami tidak menjual data pribadimu ke pihak mana pun.
  • Kami tidak mengoperasikan server yang menyimpan data gameplay-mu — tidak ada yang bisa kami bagikan di sisi kami.
  • Mitra iklan menyimpan informasi sesuai kebijakan dan kewajiban hukum mereka sendiri.

Penghapusan Data

Data lokal permainanmu tersimpan di perangkat sampai kamu menghapusnya sendiri:

  • Di dalam aplikasi: Settings & privacy → Reset all progress.
  • Hapus total: Uninstall aplikasi, atau hapus datanya lewat Settings > Apps > PuzzleSnap > Clear Data di perangkatmu.

Privasi Anak-Anak

PuzzleSnap ditujukan untuk audiens umum dan tidak dirancang khusus untuk anak-anak. Konfigurasi iklan tidak mendeklarasikan aplikasi ini sebagai child-directed.

Privasi Anak-Anak

Aplikasi ditujukan untuk pelajar SD, SMP, dan SMA. Kami tidak secara sengaja mengumpulkan data pribadi dari pengguna di bawah usia 13 tahun tanpa persetujuan orang tua. Jika kamu adalah orang tua atau wali dan menemukan adanya data anak di Aplikasi, hubungi kami untuk penghapusan.

Hak Penggunamu

  • Hapus akun & data: Logout dari Aplikasi → data lokal terhapus. Untuk hapus data Firestore, lihat langkah di bawah.
  • Hapus data lokal: Pengaturan > Aplikasi > Pintar Quiz > Hapus Data.
  • Nonaktifkan notifikasi: Pengaturan > Aplikasi > Pintar Quiz > Notifikasi.
  • Reset Advertising ID: Pengaturan > Google > Iklan.

Penghapusan Akun & Data — Pintar Quiz: Kuis SD SMP SMA

Untuk meminta penghapusan akun dan data terkait di aplikasi Pintar Quiz (zalifxstudio), ikuti langkah berikut:

  1. Kirim email ke imam.aliffirmansyah@gmail.com dengan subjek "Hapus Akun Pintar Quiz", sertakan alamat email Google yang kamu pakai login di aplikasi.

About This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Imam Alif Firmansyah (zalifxstudio) handles information when you use the Pintar Quiz: Kuis SD SMP SMA app ("App") on Android.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Data We Collect

Data TypeCollected ByStored OnSent to Server?
Advertising ID, device info, IP address, ad usage data Google AdMob Google's servers Yes, by Google
Google display name, profile photo Google Sign-In Firebase (cloud) Yes, optional
Quiz scores, quiz history, badge progress App Firebase Firestore (Google account) / Local device (guest mode) Yes (Google account)
App preferences (audio mode, theme) App Your device (SharedPreferences) No
FCM token (for study reminder notifications) Firebase Cloud Messaging Firebase servers Yes, by Firebase

Guest Mode: If you play without logging in with Google, all data is stored only on your device and never sent to any server.

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

Used to display ads (banner, interstitial, rewarded). May collect Advertising ID, device info, and usage data.

Google Privacy Policy →
🔐

Firebase (Google)

Firebase Auth for Google Sign-In, Firestore for score and leaderboard storage, Cloud Messaging for daily study reminders.

Firebase Privacy Policy →

You can opt out of ad personalization via Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization on your Android device.

Leaderboard

If you sign in with a Google account and appear on the Leaderboard, your Google display name will be shown publicly within the App. You can log out at any time from the Profile menu to switch to guest mode.

Notifications

The App sends daily study reminder notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging. You can disable notifications at any time through your Android device notification settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is designed for elementary, middle, and high school students. We do not knowingly collect personal data from users under 13 without parental consent. If you are a parent or guardian and discover your child's data in the App, please contact us for deletion.

Your Rights

  • Delete account & data: Log out from the App to clear local data. To delete Firestore data, see the steps below.
  • Clear local data: Settings > Apps > Pintar Quiz > Clear Data.
  • Disable notifications: Settings > Apps > Pintar Quiz > Notifications.
  • Reset Advertising ID: Settings > Google > Ads.

Account & Data Deletion — Pintar Quiz: Kuis SD SMP SMA

To request deletion of your account and related data in the Pintar Quiz app (zalifxstudio), follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to imam.aliffirmansyah@gmail.com with the subject "Delete Pintar Quiz Account", including the Google email you use to sign in to the app.
  2. We will verify your identity and process the deletion within a maximum of 7 business days.
  3. Data deleted: Google display name, profile photo, quiz scores, quiz history, badge progress, and FCM token stored in Firebase.
  4. Data that may be retained: aggregated/anonymized analytics logs not linked to your identity, and Advertising ID data managed directly by Google AdMob under its own policy (outside our control).
  5. Alternative without deleting your account: Logging out of the App instantly clears all local data on your device (returns to guest mode).

Leaderboard

Jika kamu memakai fitur leaderboard atau duel daring dengan akun Google, nama tampilan dan skor terkait dapat terlihat oleh pengguna lain di dalam Aplikasi. Duel daring juga menyimpan UID peserta, kode/sesi duel, kategori dan ID soal, skor, status, serta waktu sesi di Firestore. Duel melawan komputer tidak mengirim sesi duel ke Firestore.

Notifikasi

Aplikasi meminta izin notifikasi Android untuk pengingat belajar. Jika layanan push digunakan, Firebase Cloud Messaging memproses FCM device token dan Firebase menyimpan token yang dikaitkan dengan UID aplikasi. Kamu dapat menonaktifkan notifikasi lewat pengaturan Android. Saat akun Google logout, token akun dihapus; token anonim mode tamu dapat tetap tersimpan di bawah UID anonim sampai token itu diperbarui atau data tersebut dihapus.

Penyimpanan dan Retensi Data

Progres mode tamu tersimpan di perangkat sampai data Aplikasi dibersihkan melalui pengaturan Android. Data yang tersimpan di Firestore tetap ada saat logout dan diproses untuk fitur akun sampai kamu meminta penghapusannya. Token notifikasi akun Google dihapus saat logout; token anonim dapat tetap terkait dengan UID anonim sampai diperbarui atau datanya dihapus. Catatan penebusan pembelian minimum dapat disimpan untuk mencegah pembelian dikreditkan dua kali dan membantu pemeriksaan transaksi. Data diagnostik pihak ketiga mengikuti masa retensi penyedia masing-masing.

Privasi Anak-Anak

Aplikasi ditujukan untuk pelajar SD, SMP, dan SMA, termasuk anak-anak. Data akun, progres belajar, dan data perangkat yang dijelaskan dalam kebijakan ini dapat diproses saat fitur terkait digunakan. Orang tua atau wali dapat menghubungi kami untuk meminta peninjauan atau penghapusan data anak.

Hak Penggunamu

  • Logout: Mengakhiri sesi akun pada perangkat dan menghapus FCM token akun dari Firestore; logout tidak otomatis menghapus progres atau data cloud.
  • Hapus data akun: Hubungi kami melalui alamat pada bagian penghapusan akun di bawah. Data transaksi minimum dapat dipertahankan untuk mencegah penebusan ganda dan membantu penanganan pembelian.
  • Hapus data lokal: Pengaturan > Aplikasi > Pintar Quiz > Hapus Data.
  • Nonaktifkan notifikasi: Pengaturan > Aplikasi > Pintar Quiz > Notifikasi.
  • Reset Advertising ID: Pengaturan > Google > Iklan.

Penghapusan Akun & Data — PintarQuiz: School Quiz App

Untuk meminta penghapusan akun dan data terkait di aplikasi PintarQuiz: School Quiz App (Zalifx Studio), ikuti langkah berikut:

  1. Kirim email ke imam.aliffirmansyah@gmail.com dengan subjek "Hapus Akun PintarQuiz", sertakan alamat email Google yang kamu pakai login di aplikasi.
  2. Kami akan memverifikasi identitas dan memproses penghapusan dalam maksimal 7 hari kerja.
  3. Data akun yang dapat dihapus: UID dan profil Google yang tersimpan, email akun, nama/foto profil, data progres dan gamifikasi, data leaderboard/duel, status aplikasi, serta FCM token yang terkait dengan akun.
  4. Catatan transaksi: hash token pembelian, ID pesanan, produk, jumlah token, waktu, dan UID dapat disimpan terbatas untuk mencegah penebusan ulang dan membantu pemeriksaan pembelian. Nomor kartu dan kredensial pembayaran diproses oleh Google Play, bukan disimpan oleh Aplikasi.
  5. Data pihak ketiga: data iklan dan diagnostik yang dikelola Google mengikuti kebijakan serta proses penghapusan Google.
  6. Logout bukan penghapusan akun: logout hanya mengakhiri sesi di perangkat; kirim permintaan email di atas untuk meminta penghapusan data cloud.

Google Play Billing

Google Play processes payments. The App receives purchase status and product identifiers; the backend verifies token purchases with Google Play. The App does not receive card numbers or payment credentials.

Google Play purchase help →

Ad requests follow the privacy/consent choices shown by Google UMP and Google ad settings on your device. Available choices may vary by region and device configuration.

Leaderboard

If you use the leaderboard or online duel with a Google account, your display name and related score may be visible to other App users. Online duels also store participant UIDs, duel code/session, category and question IDs, scores, status, and session times in Firestore. Computer duels do not send duel sessions to Firestore.

Notifications

The App requests Android notification permission for study reminders. If push notifications are used, Firebase Cloud Messaging processes an FCM device token and Firebase stores it with the App UID. You can disable notifications in Android settings. A Google account token is removed when you log out; an anonymous guest token may remain under its anonymous UID until the token is refreshed or the data is deleted.

Data Storage and Retention

Guest progress remains on the device until App data is cleared through Android settings. Firestore data remains after logout and is processed for account features until you request deletion. A Google account notification token is removed when you log out; an anonymous guest token may remain under its anonymous UID until refreshed or deleted. Minimum purchase-redemption records may be retained to prevent duplicate credit and help investigate transactions. Third-party diagnostic data follows each provider's retention practices.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for elementary, middle, and high school students, including children. Account, learning progress, and device data described in this policy may be processed when related features are used. Parents or guardians may contact us to request review or deletion of a child's data.

Your Rights

  • Log out: Ends the account session on the device and removes the account FCM token from Firestore; logging out does not automatically delete progress or cloud data.
  • Delete account data: Contact us using the address in the deletion section below. Minimum transaction records may be retained to prevent duplicate redemption and assist purchase support.
  • Clear local data: Settings > Apps > Pintar Quiz > Clear Data.
  • Disable notifications: Settings > Apps > Pintar Quiz > Notifications.
  • Reset Advertising ID: Settings > Google > Ads.

Account & Data Deletion — PintarQuiz: School Quiz App

To request deletion of your account and related data in PintarQuiz: School Quiz App by Zalifx Studio, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to imam.aliffirmansyah@gmail.com with the subject "Delete PintarQuiz Account", including the Google email you use to sign in to the app.
  2. We will verify your identity and process the deletion within a maximum of 7 business days.
  3. Account data that can be deleted: Stored UID and Google profile, account email, display name/photo, progress and gamification data, leaderboard/duel data, app status, and FCM token linked to the account.
  4. Transaction records: A purchase-token hash, order ID, product, token amount, time, and UID may be retained as needed to prevent duplicate redemption and assist purchase reviews. Google Play processes card numbers and payment credentials; the App does not store them.
  5. Third-party data: Advertising and diagnostic data managed by Google follow Google's policies and deletion processes.
  6. Logging out is not account deletion: It only ends the device session; email the address above to request cloud-data deletion.

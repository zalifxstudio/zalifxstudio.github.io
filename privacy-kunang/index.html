GLOWFLY: NIGHT ADVENTURE

Privacy Policy  ·  Effective date: July 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Imam Alif Firmansyah ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you play Glowfly: Night Adventure (the "Game") on Android.

By playing the Game you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the Game.

1. Information We Collect

  • Local Game Data — your best score, local score history (leaderboard), coins, unlocked/rented skins, power-up inventory, sound/music settings, and language preference are stored only on your device using local storage. The Game has no user account system and no online leaderboard server.
  • App diagnostics — via Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics (see Section 3 below).

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and supported by advertising via Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads). AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier (GAID / Android Advertising ID) and other device data to serve personalised or non-personalised ads, depending on your consent and region.

We do not control the data AdMob collects. Please review Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use our partners' apps

3. Firebase Services

The Game uses the following Firebase (Google) services to keep the app stable, measure gameplay engagement, and notify you of important updates:

  • Firebase Crashlytics — automatically collects crash reports and diagnostic logs (device model, OS version, app version, stack traces) to help us fix bugs. No personal profile is created.
  • Firebase Analytics — collects anonymous gameplay events (e.g. level started, game over, ad shown) and device/usage identifiers to help us understand how the Game is played.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — used to deliver app notifications. Requires a device push token; no personal information is attached.
  • Firebase Remote Config — used only to check whether a Game update is required; no personal data is sent beyond your current app version.

See Firebase's Privacy & Security page and the Google Privacy Policy for details.

4. Your Consent Choices (GDPR / UMP)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, a consent form is shown before any ads are loaded, powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can choose between personalised and non-personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time via the Privacy options button on the Game's main menu.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — in-app advertising. See Section 2 above.
  • Firebase (Google) — Crashlytics, Analytics, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config. See Section 3 above.
  • Google Play Services — required by AdMob/Firebase on Android. Google Privacy Policy

6. How We Use Information

  • Show advertisements to support free access to the Game.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve stability (Crashlytics).
  • Understand gameplay engagement in aggregate (Analytics).
  • Notify you of important updates (Cloud Messaging) and enforce minimum required versions (Remote Config).
  • Remember your best score and settings — locally on your device only.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • Google (AdMob and Firebase), acting as described in Sections 2 and 3.
  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Local game data lives only on your device — clearing the Game's app data in Android settings, or uninstalling the Game, removes it permanently, and we hold no copy. Diagnostic/analytics data collected via Firebase is retained by Google per their standard retention policies and is not linked to a personal profile we control.

9. Children's Privacy

Glowfly: Night Adventure is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party service used by the Game, please contact us and we will assist in addressing it promptly.

10. Security

Local game data stays only on your device. The Game communicates with the internet to load advertisements and to send diagnostic/analytics data to Firebase, always over secure (HTTPS) connections. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

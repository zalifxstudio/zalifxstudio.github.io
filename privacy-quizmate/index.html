About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Imam Alif Firmansyah (zalifxstudio) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use QuizMate ("App") on Android.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Data We Collect

Data Type Collected By Stored On Sent to Server?
Name, email address, profile picture (Google Sign-In) Google / Firebase Auth Firebase servers Yes, Firebase
Username (chosen by user) App Firebase Firestore Yes, Firebase
Quiz scores, quiz history, high scores App Firebase Firestore (Google users) / Device (guests) Yes, Firebase (Google users)
Leaderboard rank & score App Firebase Firestore Yes, Firebase
FCM push notification token Firebase Cloud Messaging Firebase Firestore Yes, Firebase
App preferences (dark mode, sound, shuffle) App Your device (SharedPreferences) No
Advertising ID, device info, IP, ad usage data Google AdMob Google's servers Yes, by Google

Guest users (play without signing in) have all data stored locally on their device only. No personal information is sent to our servers when playing as a guest.

How We Use Your Data

  • Google account info — to identify you, display your name/avatar, and sync your progress across devices.
  • Quiz scores & history — to show your personal progress, performance stats, and historical results.
  • Leaderboard data — to display rankings among other players (only username and score are visible publicly).
  • FCM token — to send optional study reminder notifications. You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings.
  • Ad data — managed by Google AdMob to serve relevant advertisements.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal data to any third party.
  • Your username and leaderboard score are visible to other users within the app's leaderboard.
  • All other personal data (email, quiz history, detailed scores) is private and accessible only to you.
  • We may share data if required by law or to protect user safety.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is stored for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your data at any time:

  • In-app: Sign in → Profile → Delete Account (removes all Firestore data associated with your account).
  • Local data: Via Settings > Apps > QuizMate > Clear Data on your device.
  • By email: Contact us at the address below to request manual deletion.

Children's Privacy

QuizMate is designed for students of all ages (Primary, Middle, and High School). We do not knowingly collect personal data from users under 13 without parental consent.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data without your consent, please contact us immediately so we can delete the information.

For younger users: We recommend parents and guardians supervise account creation and enable parental controls on the device. Ad personalization can be disabled via Google Family Link.

Security

  • All data transmitted between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Firebase Firestore security rules ensure users can only read and write their own data.
  • We do not store passwords — authentication is handled entirely by Google Sign-In.

Your Rights

  • Access: View your quiz history and scores within the app.
  • Correction: Update your username within the app.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data (see Data Deletion section above).
  • Opt-out of notifications: Disable notifications via your device's Settings > Apps > QuizMate > Notifications.
  • Opt-out of ad personalization: Via Settings > Google > Ads on your device.

Cara Kami Menggunakan Data

  • Info akun Google — untuk mengidentifikasi kamu, menampilkan nama/avatar, dan menyinkronkan progres di semua perangkat.
  • Skor & riwayat kuis — untuk menampilkan perkembangan dan statistik performa pribadimu.
  • Data leaderboard — untuk menampilkan peringkat antar pemain (hanya username dan skor yang tampil publik).
  • Token FCM — untuk mengirimkan notifikasi pengingat belajar opsional. Kamu bisa menonaktifkan notifikasi kapan saja.
  • Data iklan — dikelola Google AdMob untuk menampilkan iklan yang relevan.

Berbagi Data

  • Kami tidak menjual data pribadimu ke pihak mana pun.
  • Username dan skor leaderboard terlihat oleh pengguna lain di dalam leaderboard aplikasi.
  • Data pribadi lainnya (email, riwayat kuis, skor detail) bersifat privat dan hanya dapat diakses olehmu.

Penghapusan Data

Data kamu disimpan selama akunmu aktif. Kamu bisa meminta penghapusan data kapan saja:

  • Di dalam aplikasi: Login → Profil → Hapus Akun.
  • Data lokal: Melalui Pengaturan > Aplikasi > QuizMate > Hapus Data.
  • Via email: Hubungi kami di alamat di bawah.
